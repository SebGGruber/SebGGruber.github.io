About Me

I am a machine learning engineer and statistician with a PhD in computer science and an MSc in statistics.

In my PhD at the German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ) and Goethe University Frankfurt, I developed methods for uncertainty quantification and calibration of machine learning models, from classifiers to generative models and LLMs. As a postdoc at KU Leuven, I applied this work to medical imaging. In industry, I worked at Criteo and Siemens, and since 2026 I work at LINK2AI on the safety of LLM applications.

What I Work On

  • Statistical modelling and rigorous evaluation of machine learning systems
  • Uncertainty quantification and calibration of predictions
  • Data quality: synthetic data generation, label validation, data-generating processes
  • Production ML: fine-tuning, serving, experiment tracking and cloud pipelines
  • Reliable LLM applications: hallucination detection, LLM-as-a-judge, RAG
